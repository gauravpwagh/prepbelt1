# Store listing text

## App name

PrepBelt

## Short description (80 characters max)

Plan exam prep in minutes. Review on a belt ladder from Red to Green.

## Full description

PrepBelt plans every study day for competitive exams like MPSC, UPSC and other prelims. It tells you what to review and what to read next, in the minutes you actually have.

**Reviews that fit your day**
Set a daily time, such as 90 minutes. Each day PrepBelt builds a plan: due reviews first, then new reading. Missed a few days? It offers to spread the overdue reviews over the coming days instead of burying you.

**Belts you can see**
Every chapter climbs a ladder from Red to Orange, Yellow, Light green and Green. Grade each review Forgot, Shaky or Solid, and the next date follows simple, visible rules. "Why is this due?" shows the reason.

**Always before your exam**
Add your exam dates. Reviews are pulled forward so every item gets a look before the exam day.

**Study anywhere**
Tap Start to open your PDF, link or notes app. A running clock is shown in the notification. Come back, confirm the minutes and grade it. Printed books and lectures work too.

**Capture fast**
Share a PDF, a web page or an AI chat link to PrepBelt from any app. Split a book into chapters in one step.

**Yours, on your phone**
No account, no ads, no internet permission. Daily backups go to a folder you choose. Export an exam to share its syllabus with a friend, or move it to a new phone with its progress.
