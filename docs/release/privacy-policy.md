# PrepBelt privacy policy

*Effective date: 27 October 2026*

PrepBelt is a study planner that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## What PrepBelt stores

PrepBelt stores the exams, subjects, study items, notes, links, review history and settings that you enter. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

## What PrepBelt sends

Nothing. PrepBelt has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. It cannot send your data anywhere by itself.

## Files you choose to create or share

* **Backups** are copies of the app's data. PrepBelt writes them into a folder that you pick. If that folder is synced by another app, such as a cloud drive, that app's own policy applies to the copies it syncs.
* **Exam files** (`.prepbelt.json`) are created only when you export an exam. You choose whether to share them or save them to a folder. You also choose whether they include your notes, excerpts, chat links and the names of your other exams.
* **Shared logs** are created only when you tap Share logs in Diagnostics. They contain app logs and device facts (app version, Android version, phone model, time zone, permission states). They never include your study data. They leave the phone only through the share sheet, to wherever you send them.

## Folder and file access

When you grant PrepBelt access to a folder, it lists that folder so you can link PDFs to your study items, and it writes backups into the folder you chose for them. It does not read other folders.

## Notifications

PrepBelt shows a daily reminder and, while a study timer is running, an ongoing notification. Both are created on the device.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling PrepBelt deletes everything it stored on the device. Backups and exported files that you saved to your own folders stay there until you delete them. Inside the app, Exams → Archive → Export and remove deletes an exam and its history.

## Children

PrepBelt is intended for adults preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children.
