# Tester checklist: PrepBelt 0.1.0

Thank you for testing. Please use PrepBelt for real study for at least a week. When something goes wrong, open **Settings → Diagnostics → Share logs** and send the zip with a one-line description.

Tell us your phone model and Android version, and whether battery optimisation is on for PrepBelt.

## Day 1

- [ ] Install, tap **Start fresh**, and add your exam, one subject, your daily minutes and one item. Tick "Studied today".
- [ ] Allow notifications. On the battery screen, find PrepBelt and set it to not optimised (or Unrestricted).
- [ ] Choose a backup folder, ideally one your cloud-drive app syncs.
- [ ] On Today, check that your item shows as done at **Red · 1/5**.
- [ ] Add a book and **split it into chapters**. Link a PDF from a folder you grant.
- [ ] Share a web page or an AI-chat link to PrepBelt from another app, then save it.

## Every day

- [ ] The 07:00 reminder arrives, maybe a little late. Note it if it doesn't arrive at all.
- [ ] Tap **Start** on a row: the source opens and a clock notification appears. Study, then come back to PrepBelt. The Finish sheet should be waiting.
- [ ] Grade a review. Before you tap, each grade button should show the next date.
- [ ] Try **Undo latest** on an item.

## Once during the week

- [ ] Force-stop the app during a session (Settings → Apps → PrepBelt → Force stop), then reopen it. The session should still be there.
- [ ] Swipe the session notification away, then reopen the app.
- [ ] Skip two or more days, then open the app. Check the catch-up offer and try both **Spread them** and **Undo catch-up**.
- [ ] **Exams → ⋮ → Export → Structure only → Share** to yourself, then **Exams → Import** the file.
- [ ] If you have a second phone, restore a backup on it (**Restore from backup** on the first screen), then use **Re-link by folder**.

## Things we especially want to hear

- Anything you couldn't find, or had to think twice about.
- Days when the plan felt too long or too short for your minutes.
- Any belt or due date that surprised you. "Why is this due?" on the item shows the reasoning; tell us if it didn't make sense.
